Water guide

💧 Low water

Consider drought-tolerant crops such as millets and pulses. Use mulching and efficient irrigation where suitable.

💧💧 Moderate water

Maize, pulses and selected vegetables may fit, depending on soil and season.

💧💧💧 High water

Paddy, sugarcane and banana may need reliable water and good drainage.

Seasonal guide

🌧️ Kharif

Usually aligned with the southwest monsoon; sowing windows vary by region.

🌤️ Rabi

Post-monsoon and winter crops; plan around residual moisture and irrigation.

☀️ Summer

Choose short-duration crops with a dependable irrigation plan.
